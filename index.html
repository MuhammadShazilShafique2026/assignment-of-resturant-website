McDonald's

McDonald's Pakistan is the local franchise of the international fast-food chain, launched on September 19, 1998, in Lahore, with its headquarters in Karachi. Owned and operated by SIZA Foods (a Lakson Group company), it operates over 83 outlets in 24 major cities as of 2025, offering burgers, chicken, fries, and breakfast.

Outlet in Pakistan

McDonald's operates over 70 restaurants across Pakistan, with the highest concentration of outlets located in Lahore, followed by Karachi and the Islamabad-Rawalpindi area. They offer delivery services nationwide, with major locations serving cities including Peshawar, Faisalabad, Multan, Hyderabad, Quetta, and along major motorway routes

International Head Quater of McDonald's

McDonald's global headquarters is located in Chicago, Illinois. Known as "MHQ," the corporate office is situated at 110 N. Carpenter St., Chicago, Illinois 60607, USA. This location serves as the central hub for the company's worldwide operations, focusing on innovation, collaboration, and training for its global franchise network
